Twenty CRM is the central record of truth for every Luxurique deal, client, and company. Every tool in the cluster — n8n automation, Supabase analytics, Preset.io dashboards, Vero email marketing — either reads from or writes to Twenty. You access it at [luxurique.twenty.com](https://luxurique.twenty.com).

## Core record types

Twenty organises your data into three main record types.

**People** — individual contacts: clients, agents, suppliers, and other contacts associated with a deal or company.

**Companies** — organisations: client companies, travel agencies, DMCs, hotels, and other suppliers.

**Deals** — active and historical deal records. In the Twenty interface these appear as **Deals**; in the underlying API they are called **Opportunities** (`opportunity`). You will see the API name referenced in some automation and integration contexts, but in the CRM UI you always work with Deals.

<Note>
  The Twenty API name for a deal is `opportunity`. If you ever see that term in a Slack notification, n8n log, or technical note, it refers to the same deal record you see in the CRM.
</Note>

## Navigating to a record

Every record in Twenty has a direct link in the format:

```
https://luxurique.twenty.com/object/<type>/<recordId>
```

For example, a direct link to a Person record might look like:
`https://luxurique.twenty.com/object/person/abc123`

You can share these links with colleagues to point them directly to a specific deal or contact.

## Linking a QC project to a deal

The Quotation Costing (QC) Webapp can pull client and deal information directly from Twenty, and push costing results back — eliminating double-entry between the two tools.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project in the QC Webapp">
    Navigate to your project in the QC Webapp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the project to a Twenty deal">
    Use the deal-link field in the QC Webapp to connect the project to the corresponding Twenty Deal record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pulled fields">
    Once linked, the QC Webapp automatically pulls in the following from Twenty:
    - **Client name**
    - **Deal name**
    - **Deal stage**
    - **Sales Lead**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the quotation">
    When you export the completed quotation, the QC Webapp pushes the following values back to the linked deal in Twenty:
    - **Grand total**
    - **Gross cost**
    - **PAX** (passenger count)
    - **Travel dates**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Linking a QC project to a deal saves time and keeps your CRM data current without manual updates. Make a habit of linking early — before you start costing — so the deal fields are always available in the QC Webapp.
</Tip>

## How deals get created and updated

Deals in Twenty come from two sources:

- **Automated (n8n):** When a business inquiry arrives in the shared inbox, n8n classifies it, extracts the relevant CRM fields, and creates a Person, Company, and Deal in Twenty automatically. The deal usually appears within a few minutes of the email arriving.
- **Manual:** You can create or edit any record directly in Twenty CRM at any time.

<Warning>
  The n8n automation is **create-only** — it creates new records but never overwrites values you have edited manually. Once you update a field in Twenty, that field is yours to manage. Automation will not touch it.
</Warning>

## Editing and correcting records

If an automatically created record is missing information, misclassified, or linked to the wrong company:

1. Open the record in Twenty CRM.
2. Edit the fields you need to correct.
3. Save. Your changes are permanent and will not be overwritten by automation.

<Note>
  If a contact is missing entirely — for example, a client who emailed from a personal Gmail address — you can create them manually in Twenty. See the [Email Triage](/crm/email-triage) page for known gaps in automatic contact creation.
</Note>

## Company and person types

Twenty uses a **Type** field on both Companies and People to indicate their role in a deal:

- **CLIENT** — the end customer for whom the trip is arranged.
- **AGENCY** — a travel intermediary (travel agency, DMC, incentive house) that is arranging the trip on behalf of a client.

A record can hold more than one type. Types are assigned at creation from the first email that creates the record. If a record's type is wrong, update it directly in Twenty.
