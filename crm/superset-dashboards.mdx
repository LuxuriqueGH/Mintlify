Luxurique uses **Preset.io** — a hosted, managed version of Apache Superset — as its sole business intelligence tool. Preset.io draws from the analytics data captured from Twenty CRM activity and gives you aggregate reporting across the entire deal pipeline. You access Preset.io through the Luxurique Preset workspace. If you do not yet have access, contact a Supervisor to request it.

## What the dashboards show

The Preset.io dashboards draw from aggregate analytics functions built on top of the Supabase activity log. Every chart reflects live CRM activity as Twenty records are created and updated.

## What you can and cannot see

Preset.io connects to the analytics data using a read-only, restricted connection that can only access aggregate summary functions — it physically cannot read per-deal rows, individual deal names, margins, or costs. You will always see summary numbers, never raw CRM records.

<Note>
  Individual deal margins, contract amounts, and cost data are **not** exposed in Preset.io. If you need deal-level figures, open the record directly in Twenty CRM at [luxurique.twenty.com](https://luxurique.twenty.com).
</Note>

## How the data stays current

Every create, update, or delete in Twenty CRM fires a webhook that Supabase captures in an append-only activity log. The Preset.io dashboards read aggregate functions that sit on top of that log. There is no manual refresh step — data flows through automatically as CRM activity happens.

A background reconcile process also runs every ten minutes to recover any webhooks that were dropped before they could be recorded. This means the dashboards remain accurate even under transient delivery failures.

## Getting access

<Tip>
  Access to the Luxurique Preset workspace is provisioned by a Supervisor. Send your request with your Luxurique email address and the Supervisor will add you to the workspace.
</Tip>

Once you have access, navigate to the Luxurique Preset workspace in your browser and sign in. All published dashboards are available from the **Dashboards** menu.

## Reporting a data gap

If a chart appears to be missing recent data or a deal you know exists does not appear in a report, it is possible that one or more webhook deliveries were dropped before the reconcile process could recover them. In that case, a manual backfill can be requested from the engineering team to replay Twenty's event history and fill the gap.

<Warning>
  Do not attempt to edit data in Preset.io directly. All source data lives in Twenty CRM. Changes made only in a dashboard tool will be overwritten the next time the underlying data refreshes.
</Warning>
