Luxurique is planning an integration between **Asana** and **Twenty CRM** that will connect project management work to deal activity automatically. When a deal reaches a specified stage in Twenty CRM, the integration will create a corresponding Asana project pre-populated with deal context. A read-only deal mirror will keep the project team informed of the latest CRM state, and any field edits made in Asana will surface as tasks for the Sales Lead or Project Lead to action in Twenty CRM.

<Note>
  This integration is currently in the planning stage and is not yet active. No Asana projects are being created automatically, and no CRM data is being synced to Asana at this time. This page will be updated when the integration launches.
</Note>

## How edits will flow

The edit-to-task loop is a key design principle of this integration. When a Project Lead notices that deal information in Asana needs updating, they edit the relevant field in Asana. That edit does **not** write to Twenty CRM automatically. Instead, the integration creates a task assigned to the Sales Lead, who then makes the change in Twenty CRM. Once the Sales Lead updates the record in Twenty, the mirror in Asana reflects the new value on the next sync.

This one-way write boundary means Twenty CRM always remains the authoritative record — Asana never overwrites CRM data.

<Tip>
  If you are a Sales Lead and receive an Asana task requesting a CRM update, make the change on the deal record in Twenty CRM at [luxurique.twenty.com](https://luxurique.twenty.com). The Asana mirror will update automatically after you save.
</Tip>
