Luxurique runs on a cluster of seven integrated tools. At the centre sits **Twenty CRM**, where every client, supplier, and deal record lives. Around it, n8n automates email triage, Supabase captures every record change for analytics, and Preset.io (Superset) surfaces that data as business-intelligence dashboards. Vero handles outbound email marketing, Asana manages project tasks, and SupplierDB was used to migrate supplier records from SharePoint into Twenty. You interact with most of this stack through Twenty CRM and Slack — the automation runs silently in the background.

## How data flows through the cluster

Data enters the cluster through two main paths: incoming emails and direct edits in Twenty CRM.

**Email path:**
1. An email arrives in the Luxurique shared inbox and is copied to the M365 proxy inbox.
2. **n8n** reads the proxy inbox, classifies the email, and extracts CRM fields (contact name, company, deal details).
3. n8n writes Person, Company, and Deal records into **Twenty CRM** via GraphQL upsert.
4. Twenty fires a webhook for every record create or update.
5. **Supabase** receives those webhooks and appends each change to an immutable activity log, then derives analytics views.
6. **Preset.io** reads the aggregate analytics from Supabase and updates the dashboards.

**Manual path:**
Any record created or edited directly in Twenty CRM follows the same webhook → Supabase → Preset chain from step 4 onward.

<Note>
  You do not need to enter most inbound leads manually. They appear in Twenty CRM within minutes of the email arriving. If a record looks wrong, edit it directly in Twenty — automation will not overwrite your changes.
</Note>

## Supplier data

Supplier records (companies and contacts) were migrated from SharePoint Lists into Twenty CRM as a one-off project using **SupplierDB**. That migration is complete. New supplier records should be added directly in Twenty CRM.
