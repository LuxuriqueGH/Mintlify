Comments let you leave notes, ask questions, and flag issues directly on the parts of a QC that matter — without resorting to external email threads or chat messages that quickly lose context. You can comment at any level of the QC structure, mention colleagues by name to alert them, and resolve threads when the discussion is done.

## Where you can comment

You can attach a comment to any of the following:

- The **QC** itself (the whole quotation)
- A **tab**
- A **section**
- A **group**
- An individual **row (line item)**

For row-level comments, you can also **pin the comment to a specific grid column** — useful when your note is about a particular field value, such as a cost figure or a markup override.

## Mentioning a colleague

Type **@** followed by a colleague's name to mention them in a comment body. The app displays a picker as you type so you can select the right person. Once you post the comment, the mentioned colleague receives an **inbox notification** immediately. They can click through from their inbox directly to the comment.

<Note>
  You can mention multiple colleagues in a single comment. Each person receives their own inbox notification.
</Note>

## Thread structure

Comments are organised into threads. Every thread has a **root comment** — the first message posted to a particular location. Replies attach directly to the root.

<Warning>
  Threads are **one level deep only**. You can reply to a root comment, but you cannot reply to a reply. If you want to continue a sub-discussion, reply to the root and quote or reference the earlier reply in your text.
</Warning>

## Editing and deleting your own comments

You can **edit** or **delete** any comment you authored, as long as it has not been redacted by a Supervisor. When a comment is deleted, its text is removed but the thread structure is preserved so that any replies remain readable in context.

## Supervisor redaction

Supervisors can **redact any comment** on any QC, regardless of who wrote it. Redaction removes the comment body in the same way as an author delete. Use redaction when a comment contains information that should not remain on the record — for example, sensitive supplier details or an error that could cause confusion.

## Resolving threads

Once a discussion is concluded, you can mark the thread as **resolved**. Resolved threads are hidden from the default comment view but remain accessible if you choose to show them. Only the thread root can be resolved — not individual replies.

<Tip>
  Resolve threads as soon as the underlying question or issue is addressed. This keeps the active comment list focused on open items and makes it easier for colleagues to spot new discussions.
</Tip>

## The inbox

Your **inbox** collects two types of notification:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mentions" icon="at">
    Posted when a colleague uses @your-name in a comment body.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Replies" icon="reply">
    Posted when someone replies to a thread you started.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The inbox badge in the app navigation shows the count of unread mentions and replies. Click any inbox item to jump directly to the relevant comment in its QC.

<Note>
  If you lose access to a QC — for example, because your project assignment is removed — the corresponding inbox items for that QC are automatically deleted. You will not see notifications for comments on QCs you can no longer open.
</Note>

## Adding a comment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the comment panel for your target">
    Right-click or open the row menu on the item you want to comment on (QC, tab, section, group, or row). Select **Add comment** or open the comments panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a column pin (row comments only)">
    If you are commenting on a row and your note relates to a specific column, select that column from the pin picker. This is optional — leave it blank for a general row comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your comment">
    Type your message in the comment field. Use **@name** to mention colleagues. Comments must be between 1 and 4,000 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post">
    Click **Post** or press the keyboard shortcut to submit. Mentioned colleagues are notified immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
