Each row in a quote-set tab's cost table is a **cost line item** — one discrete service, product, or expense in your itinerary. You build a quotation by filling in a set of rows, activating them with the **Include** checkbox, and letting the QC Sheet calculate the pricing automatically.

## Key columns

The table below covers the columns you work with most often. Formula and system columns (Item ID, Version, group ordering columns, etc.) are calculated automatically — you do not edit them directly.

| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Item** | A short description of the service or product (e.g. "Airport transfer", "Gala dinner"). |
| **Date** | The service date. Required before you can activate the row. |
| **Category** | The cost category that drives markup (e.g. "Accommodation", "Transport"). Drawn from the MarkupMaster rate matrix. |
| **Label** | The sub-classification within the chosen Category (e.g. "Hotel – 5 Star", "Private Transfer"). The available options update automatically when you set the Category. |
| **City** | The city or destination where the service takes place. Required for activation. |
| **Qty A / Unit A** | Primary quantity and its unit (e.g. 2 nights, 1 vehicle). |
| **Qty B / Unit B** | Secondary quantity and unit where a service has two dimensions (e.g. 10 guests at 1 meal each). |
| **Estimated Unit Cost** | Your initial cost estimate before supplier confirmation. |
| **Supplier Quote Unit Cost** | The unit cost as quoted by the supplier. Overrides the estimate in pricing once filled in. |
| **Final Paid Unit Cost** | The actual amount paid after the service is delivered. Used for reconciliation. |
| **Status** | The current state of the line item (e.g. confirmed, pending, **Cancelled**). |
| **Supplier** | The supplier name for this item. |
| **Detail** | Free-text notes about the item — extra context for the team, not shown on the client quotation. |

<Note>
You only need to fill in the cost column relevant to your current stage. Use **Estimated Unit Cost** for early planning, **Supplier Quote Unit Cost** once you have a confirmed price from the supplier, and **Final Paid Unit Cost** after payment.
</Note>

## Activating a row with the Include checkbox

Filling in the columns above does not automatically make a row part of the quotation. To activate a row — which registers it in the system, generates its unique **Item ID**, and includes it in pricing calculations — you tick the **Include** checkbox (column F).

Here is what happens when you tick Include:

1. The QC Sheet validates that the row has the minimum required fields: **Date**, **City**, **Qty**, **Label**, and **Costing Basis**.
2. If validation passes, a unique **Item ID** is generated for the row in the format `ProjectID-SubID-RandomToken`. This ID is stable and persists for the lifetime of the row.
3. The row becomes a "1 of 1" group and is incorporated into the table's sort order.
4. The row contributes to the tab's subtotal and the Overview grand total.

<Warning>
If you tick Include and nothing happens (no Item ID appears), check that the **Project ID** and **Sub ID** have been set on the Overview sheet, and that all required fields (Date, City, Qty, Label, Costing Basis) are filled in. The row will not activate until all are present.
</Warning>

To remove a row from the quotation without deleting it, untick the Include checkbox. The row stays in the table but no longer contributes to any totals.

## Cancelled status

Setting a row's **Status** to `Cancelled` has a special effect: the row is automatically moved to a dedicated **Cancelled** section at the bottom of the table. This happens automatically — you do not need to move the row yourself.

Cancelled rows remain visible in the table for record-keeping but are visually separated from active items and do not contribute to the quotation output. The Cancelled section cannot be deleted or reorganised manually.

<Tip>
Use the Cancelled status rather than deleting a row when you want to keep a paper trail of services that were considered but not included in the final itinerary.
</Tip>

## The Detail field

The **Detail** column is a free-text field for internal notes. Use it to record anything that helps the team understand the line item: supplier contact notes, special instructions, alternative options considered, or any other context.

Detail text does not appear on the client-facing PDF or Word quotation — it is for internal use only. There is no character limit, but keep entries concise so the table remains easy to scan.
