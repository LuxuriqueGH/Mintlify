The Overview sheet is the top-level summary for your entire QC file. It shows you where the project stands financially across every active tab, along with the payment schedule for the client. You will typically check Overview when you need a quick read on total costs, margins, or upcoming payment milestones — without having to open each costing tab individually.

## OverviewTable: per-tab cost summary

The **OverviewTable** sits at the centre of the Overview sheet. It contains one row per active costing tab, automatically populated from the data in each tab's MainTable. The table gives you a side-by-side comparison across all tabs.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Tab** | The display name of the costing tab (e.g. "Tab 1", "Tab 2") |
| **Cost** | Sum of raw Total Cost for included items on that tab |
| **Marked Up Cost** | Sum of post-markup costs for included items |
| **PAXDA** | Per-person, per-day rate for the tab |
| **Subtotal Price** | Client-facing subtotal price for the tab |

Totals rows at the bottom of the table aggregate across all tabs. The grand total figures that feed the PDF quotation — **QUOTEPRICE**, **GRANDTOTAL**, and **PERPERSON** — are all derived from these Overview totals.

<Note>
If a tab has been marked **Excluded** in its Tab Details labels selector, it contributes zero to OverviewTable and to all global totals. An excluded tab will not appear in the PDF or Word export either.
</Note>

## Project-level fields

Above the OverviewTable you will find the project metadata fields that flow through to every export:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Project & Client" icon="user">
    **PROJECTNAME**, **CLIENTNAME**, **PIC** (person in charge), and **PAX** (traveller count) are entered here and referenced by both the PDF and Word export pipelines.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dates & Duration" icon="calendar">
    **STARTDATE**, **ENDDATE**, and the calculated **DURATION** field. These drive date formatting on the quotation cover and per-person/per-day calculations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fees & Rates" icon="percent">
    **COORDFEE**, **TAX**, **PMNTFEE**, **MARGIN**, and **COMMRATE** are set here. Non-zero fee values cause the matching fee rows (Coordination, Tax, Payment) to appear automatically on the PDF quotation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Totals" icon="sigma">
    **SUBPRICE**, **QUOTEPRICE**, **TAXAMOUNT**, **GRANDTOTAL**, and **PERPERSON** are calculated here and consumed directly by the quotation rebuild and all three export formats.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## PaymentTable: payment schedule

The **PaymentTable** sits below the OverviewTable on the same Overview sheet. It holds the payment schedule for this specific quotation — the deposit, progress payments, and balance — including due dates, amounts, and payment tracking (Amount Paid, Date Paid).

You can populate the PaymentTable manually, or apply a reusable template from the PaymentMaster sheet.

<Tip>
To apply a payment policy template, select a policy from the **Select Policy** dropdown at the top of the PaymentTable. The amounts and due dates will populate automatically based on the GRANDTOTAL and STARTDATE for this quotation.
</Tip>

## PaymentMaster: reusable payment policy templates

The **PaymentMaster** sheet holds a library of pre-configured payment policies (`tPaymentMaster` table). Each policy defines a structured set of payment rows — deposit percentage, progress payment schedule, balance timing — that you can apply to any QC in one step via the PaymentTable dropdown.

<Note>
PaymentMaster is a shared reference sheet. Changes you make to a policy template there will affect how it populates on future quotations, but they do not retroactively update any PaymentTable that has already been applied.
</Note>

## Overview as the single source of truth

Everything on the Overview sheet flows downstream. The quotation rebuild reads fee values, project metadata, and global totals from here. The PDF, Word, and XLSX exports all reference the same named ranges — **PROJECTNAME**, **GRANDTOTAL**, **PERPERSON**, and so on — that point back to Overview cells. Keeping the Overview fields up to date before you export ensures that every output is consistent.
